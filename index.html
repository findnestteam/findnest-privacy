FINDNEST Privacy Policy

Everything you own. Nothing you lose.

Effective date: November 27, 2026

1. Introduction

FINDNEST is a personal home inventory application developed by FINDNEST Team. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use FINDNEST.

FINDNEST is designed with privacy in mind. Your inventory is stored locally on your device. We do not operate an account system or a cloud service for storing your inventory.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

When you use FINDNEST, you may enter information such as:

  • Item names and descriptions
  • Categories and storage locations
  • Notes about your belongings
  • Photos of your items

This information is stored locally on your device and is used to provide the app's inventory management features. It is not uploaded to FINDNEST servers.

3. Photos and Device Permissions

FINDNEST allows you to take or select photos of your items. Camera access is used when you choose to take a photo. Photo library or media access is used when you choose an existing image.

Photos are stored locally on your device for use within your inventory. FINDNEST does not upload your item photos to its own servers.

4. Data Sharing and Collection

FINDNEST does not require registration, an account, an email address, or a user profile.

The app does not send your inventory information, item descriptions, notes, or photos to FINDNEST servers. We do not sell your personal information.

Payments and in-app purchases are processed through Google Play. Google may process purchase and payment information under its own privacy policy. FINDNEST does not receive or store your full payment card details.

5. In-App Purchases

FINDNEST offers 10 free item spaces. You may optionally purchase additional item spaces through Google Play:

  • 30 Extra Item Spaces - €1.99
  • 100 Extra Item Spaces - €4.99

These are one-time purchases. The same product may be purchased again to obtain additional item spaces.

Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. Purchase processing is subject to Google's terms and privacy practices. The remaining additional item spaces are maintained locally by the app.

6. Backups and Data Transfers

FINDNEST provides an encrypted backup and transfer feature. Backup files are created at your request and are protected using encryption.

You choose where to save or transfer your backup file. FINDNEST does not automatically upload backup files to a cloud service.

You are responsible for keeping your backup files and their passwords safe. If you lose your device or delete the app without a backup, locally stored information may be lost.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Inventory information remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

FINDNEST does not maintain a remote inventory database or user account that requires deletion on our servers.

Backup files saved outside the app must be deleted by you from the location where you stored them.

8. Data Security

FINDNEST is designed to keep inventory information on your device. Backup and transfer files use encryption to help protect your information.

You are responsible for securing your device, using a strong device lock, and protecting your backup passwords.

9. Children's Privacy

FINDNEST is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not require users to create accounts or submit personal details to FINDNEST.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features or privacy practices change. The latest version will always be available at this page, together with its effective date.
